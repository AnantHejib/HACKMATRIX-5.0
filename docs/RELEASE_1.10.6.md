# FIN 1.10.6

FIN 1.10.6 introduces a redesigned, motion-conscious application loader.

## Added

- A winding financial route that carries a dollar through the analysis journey.
- A dollar-to-paper-plane transformation followed by an upward launch.
- A synchronized loading indicator and updated startup message.
- A one-shot sequence that hands off cleanly to the dashboard.

## Performance and accessibility

- The moving elements use compositor-friendly transforms and opacity.
- The loader avoids expensive continuous blur and layout animation.
- Devices requesting reduced motion receive a short static transition.
- The loader remains tappable so users can enter the app immediately.

## Release identity

- Product version: `1.10.6`
- Android version code: `18`
