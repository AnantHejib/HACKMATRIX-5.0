The product

More clarity at every financial decision.

FIN connects the evidence in your statement with the choices you make next.

◎

Understand your financial health

See how recorded income and spending connect to your cash reserves and debt. Each health dimension explains its meaning, calculation and the next priority.

What does the score mean?

With declared balances, FIN evaluates cash flow, liquidity, debt and stability, with credit information where available. A statement-only view uses the evidence present in the file. Missing dimensions are excluded rather than silently treated as poor performance. The FIN score is not an official credit score.

⇧

Import your real history

Upload CSV or text-based PDF statements, including password-protected files. FIN extracts dated payments locally and structures them for analysis.

What should I verify?

Review descriptions, amounts and categories. A GPay incoming transfer is not automatically treated as earned salary. Statements may omit other accounts or opening cash, so declare verified income and balances where needed. Image-only scanned PDFs require OCR.

⌁

See patterns and obligations

Understand which payees or categories absorb your spending and which payments repeat. Inspect debt pressure and a 30-day cash-flow outlook.

How are patterns detected?

FIN examines payment cadence and amount consistency to infer recurring obligations. Spending trends combine historical direction and recent behavior. Inferred subscriptions and categories should be reviewed; the debt analysis depends on the obligations and balances you provide.

6M

Compare three six-month plans

Choose between balanced progress, stronger cash protection and additional debt repayment. Every strategy shows six monthly periods against your confirmed reserve.

What does each strategy prioritize?

Balanced momentum distributes affordable optional actions. Stability shield prioritizes reserve protection. Debt release directs more available cash toward debt reduction. Essentials and required debt payments are protected before optional spending, savings and extra repayments. Conflicts and proposed goal relaxations are shown explicitly.

±

Understand what changes

Add a transaction or update cash, savings or debt. FIN recalculates the model and shows a before-and-after comparison of the financial outlook.

Try an unexpected expense

Add a clearly labelled medical-expense example in the change simulator. Compare safe-to-spend, the health score and available cash-gap estimates. The recommendation may change, or retain its direction with a different urgency or expected impact. Planner stress controls also simulate income reductions and essential shocks.

◇

Make uncertainty visible

See the difference between recorded facts, possible future outcomes and proposed actions. Forecasts include ranges and explain when evidence is insufficient.

How should I read the ranges?

P10–P90 is the central 80% of simulated outcomes under the model's assumptions. Six-month risks come from 500 conditional simulation paths and are not historically calibrated. Eligible 30-day forecasts are compared with later outcomes and an average baseline using error, interval coverage and width.

✦

Ask questions in ordinary language

Ask FIN about spending, affordability or saving priorities using your imported financial context. The interface shows which response mode is active.

Which assistant is responding?

Local evidence answers are available in the web experience. Android can use a separately installed on-device model. Cloud responses require a configured service and explicit sharing choice. A fluent answer does not replace checking its evidence and assumptions.

↗

Inspect the impact of an action

Compare recommendations by their estimated cash released, reserve growth or debt reduction, with a timeframe and supporting assumptions.

Why show the calculation?

An instruction such as “reduce spending” is more useful when you can see the expected effect. FIN's action center provides calculation details and lets you accept, complete or dismiss actions. Outcomes remain estimates; interest, fees and changing behavior may not be captured.

अ

Use a familiar language

Switch between English, Hindi and Marathi, including localized numeral display. Keep your analysis available in the language you choose.

Where does my data live?

Web financial records stay in the current browser. Android records stay on the device. Your login works across both experiences, but records do not automatically sync. Some detailed explanations can retain an English fallback.

The reasoning behind the interface

Know what is measured, estimated and suggested.

FIN's explanations connect each result to its source. That makes it easier to judge whether the recommendation fits your situation.

Three layers of a financial decision

01 · Observed or declared facts

Your statement records payments; you separately declare cash and debt that the statement cannot establish. A payment total is evidence from that file, not a complete picture of every account.

02 · Model predictions

History informs a range of possible future balances. Variability and assumptions matter. When current cash or sufficient history is missing, FIN withholds unsupported risk estimates.

03 · Recommended actions

FIN proposes actions that fit the available evidence and constraints. Each action explains its expected effect and timeframe so you can compare choices before deciding.
