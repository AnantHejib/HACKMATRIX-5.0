The product

More clarity at every financial decision.

FIN connects the evidence in your statement with the choices you make next.

◎

Understand your financial health

Bring transactions and declared balances together. Explore each dimension, its calculation and what deserves attention.

⇧

Import your real history

Process CSV and text-based PDF statements locally, including password-protected PDFs. Review transactions and correct categories.

⌁

See patterns and obligations

Explore spending concentrations, recurring payments, debt pressure and possible future cash-flow gaps.

6M

Compare three six-month plans

Review Balanced momentum, Stability shield and Debt release against essential expenses, debt obligations and a declared reserve.

±

Understand what changes

Add income, an expense or a debt update. Compare before-and-after metrics and understand the resulting recommendation.

◇

Make uncertainty visible

Distinguish facts, predictions and recommendations. Inspect forecast ranges, missing information and eligible historical validation.

Behind the experience

Transparent methods. Connected experiences.

Built by Ctrl Alt Elite for Hackmatrix 5.0. FIN shares its analysis and planning interface across Android and the browser.

Android + WebLocal PDF extractionMonte Carlo simulationRolling historical backtestsPostgreSQL authenticationEnglish / Hindi / Marathi
Financial records stay in the browser on web and on the device on Android; they do not automatically sync. Six-month risks are conditional simulations and are not historically calibrated. Thirty-day validation is shown when history is sufficient. Scanned PDF images need OCR. FIN provides educational guidance, not investment advice.
